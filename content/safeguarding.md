The [BMABA](https://bmaba.org.uk/) are NRBJJ's registered association and Designated Safeguarding Lead (DSL). 

If you have any concerns about safeguarding or conduct, please initially report to [Karl Hendrick](mailto:karl.hendrick@gmail.com). 

In the event you do not recieve a prompt response, have concerns about the club response, or if the concern relates to this person: you can escalate to BMABA's professional [safeguarding team](mailto:safeguarding@bmaba.org.uk).
